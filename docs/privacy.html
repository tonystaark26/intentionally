Intentionally: Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Intentionally is a Mass book and personal account app for priests. This page explains what happens to the information you enter.

Your entries stay on your phone

Mass intentions, stipends, notes, accounts and transactions are stored only on your own device. There is no Intentionally server, and nobody else can see your entries.

Google sign-in and Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks for two things only:

  • Your email address, so the app can show which Google account holds your backup.
  • Its own app folder on your Google Drive (drive.appdata), where it saves one backup file. This hidden folder can only be read by Intentionally. The app cannot see, change or delete any of your other Drive files.

The backup goes straight from your phone to your Google Drive. It is not sent anywhere else, not shared, and not used for advertising.

Removing your data

To remove the Drive backup and the app's access, open your Google Account, go to Security > Your connections to third-party apps & services, choose Intentionally and remove access. You can also delete the hidden app data from Google Drive under Settings > Manage apps. To remove the entries on the phone, clear the app's site data or uninstall it.
